Frequently Asked Questions

What vintage jacket brands do you carry?

We carry a wide range of vintage jackets with a strong focus on Carhartt workwear (Detroit jackets, hooded bombers, canvas work jackets), The North Face fleece, Adidas varsity jackets, and vintage sports bombers. Inventory changes regularly as new finds come in.

Are the vintage jackets in good condition?

Condition is described in detail on every listing. Many jackets show honest vintage wear — natural fading, surface wear, or patina consistent with their age. Any significant flaws are clearly photographed and noted. We never misrepresent condition.

What sizes are available?

Our vintage jacket inventory ranges from Small to 2XL and up, depending on current stock. All listings include the original size tag plus measurements where possible. Check individual item pages for exact sizing.

Do you ship vintage jackets nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all 50 US states. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout. Most orders ship within 1–2 business days from Portland, Oregon.
