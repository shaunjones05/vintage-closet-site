About The Vintage Closet

The Vintage Closet is a curated vintage clothing shop based in Portland, Oregon, focused on timeless workwear, denim, graphic tees, and statement outerwear. Every piece is hand-picked with intention â€” selected for quality, character, and everyday wearability rather than trends or fast fashion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of vintage clothing do you carry?

We carry a curated selection of vintage jackets, graphic tees, crewnecks, hoodies, and pants. We specialize in 80s and 90s Carhartt workwear, vintage band tees, and other hand-picked secondhand clothing.

Do you ship vintage clothing nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all 50 US states. Shipping is a flat $5 on every order, regardless of size. Most orders ship within 1–2 business days.

What condition are the vintage items in?

Condition varies by item and is always clearly described on each product page. Most pieces show honest vintage wear — natural fading, pilling, or signs of age consistent with their era. We never misrepresent condition.

Is it safe to buy here?

Yes. All purchases are processed through Stripe, one of the world’s most trusted payment platforms. Your card details are encrypted and never stored by us. Stripe’s buyer protections apply to every transaction.
