Frequently Asked Questions

Are your vintage band tees authentic?

Yes. All band tees and graphic tees we sell are authentic vintage pieces — not reprints or reproductions. Single-stitch construction, period-correct tags (Fruit of the Loom, Hanes, Screen Stars), and age-consistent fading help verify authenticity. Each listing includes photos of the tag and any flaws.

What condition are the vintage graphic tees in?

Condition varies by item and is clearly described on each product page. Vintage tees typically show natural fading, light pilling, or minor graphic cracking consistent with age. We photograph all flaws and describe condition honestly.

What bands and styles do you carry?

Our inventory rotates regularly. We specialize in 80s and 90s band tees (Grateful Dead, The Smiths, AC/DC, Joy Division, Harley-Davidson), vintage 90s corporate graphic tees (Nike, Levi's), and rare AOP (all-over print) shirts. New items are added regularly.

Do you ship vintage tees nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all 50 US states. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout. Most orders ship within 1–2 business days.
