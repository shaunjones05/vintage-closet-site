Frequently Asked Questions

Are your Carhartt jackets authentic vintage?

Yes. Every Carhartt piece we sell is individually sourced from estate sales, thrift stores, and private sellers. All items are verified genuine vintage, typically from the 80s or 90s — never modern reproductions.

What condition are the vintage Carhartt jackets in?

Condition is clearly described on every item listing. Most of our Carhartt pieces show honest workwear patina — natural fading, wear, and character consistent with their age. We photograph all flaws and never misrepresent condition.

Do you ship vintage Carhartt items nationwide?

Yes, we ship all items within the United States. Shipping is available to all 50 states and calculated at checkout.

What sizes do your vintage Carhartt jackets come in?

Our Carhartt inventory includes a range of sizes, typically Large, XL, and 2XL. Each listing includes the specific size tag and chest/sleeve measurements. Check individual listings for details.
